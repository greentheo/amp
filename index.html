Measure lag

Plays five short chirps out of the selected output and listens for them on the selected input, timed on the amp's own clock. It measures the delay a note actually suffers, not what the browser claims.

Make a loop first. Turn the volume down before you connect anything.

  • Your interface — a cable from its output (or headphone out) back into the input you play through. Set both In and Out to the interface. This is the path your guitar takes.
  • No cables — set In to the MacBook microphone and Out to the MacBook speakers. Only a rough check: the built-in mic and speakers add about 48 ms of their own processing that your guitar path never goes through, so expect a big number.
